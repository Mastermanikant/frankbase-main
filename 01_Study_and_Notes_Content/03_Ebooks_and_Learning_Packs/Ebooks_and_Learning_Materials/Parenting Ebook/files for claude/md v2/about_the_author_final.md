## About the Author

My name is Master Manikant.

As the eldest member of my family, I have spent years guiding and teaching my younger siblings. I closely observed how family behavior, communication, and treatment shape a child’s thinking and emotions.

As a teacher, I also observe students daily—how different teaching styles and behavior from adults affect their confidence, learning, and personality. These real-life observations helped me understand what truly works with children and why.

This book is based on those real experiences—what I saw, what I applied, and what actually made a difference.
